Databricks · 2023–2026

Cloud storage infrastructure

Scaling storage provisioning and credentials across AWS, Azure, and GCP.

55×fewer DB transactions
35×less lock time

1Mcustomers in load tests
The engineering details
Prepared a storage control plane for a step-change in traffic.

Load-tested the system into six-figure bucket counts and million-customer scenarios, then removed query, polling, and refresh bottlenecks. P95 latency fell from roughly 2 seconds to about 400 ms in testing, then stayed below 200 ms at peak launch traffic.

Collapsed repeated work into a coordinated deployment path.

Redesigned bucket deployment coordination to eliminate redundant database work and lock contention. The change cut transaction volume by about 55×, reduced lock time by about 35×, and shortened reconciliation from roughly 65 seconds to 29 seconds.

Turned slow fleet-wide checks into targeted decisions.

Added a lightweight fullness signal for storage buckets so assignment could avoid expensive fleet scans. Refresh time dropped from hours to seconds while assignment latency stayed under 300 ms at 100k-scale tests.
